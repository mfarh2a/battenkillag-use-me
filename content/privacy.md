# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Battenkill Ag Services, LLC ("Battenkill Ag," "we," "us" or "our") helps farms with the H-2A program. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through battenkillag.com and our customer portal (the "Services"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us:**

- **Checklist download:** your name, email and, if you choose, your farm name.
- **Contact form and quote requests:** your name, farm name, email, phone, state and what you tell us about your season.
- **Customers:** account login details, business information such as farm location, crops, job details, wages, housing and payroll records, and documents you upload to the portal. These documents may include personal information about workers, such as names, dates of birth, passport details and employment records.
- **Payments:** we accept online payments and checks. Online payments are handled by our third-party payment processor. We do not see or store your full card or bank account numbers.

**Information collected automatically:** IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and similar usage data, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

## 2. How we use information

- to answer your request and prepare a quote
- to prepare, file and support your H-2A applications
- to run the customer portal, including document drop boxes, invoices and payments
- to send invoices, payment reminders and important notices about your case, by email or text
- to send the checklist and occasional H-2A tips (you can unsubscribe at any time)
- to keep the Services secure and prevent fraud
- to respond to government agencies when the law requires it, such as a Department of Labor audit

## 3. How we share information

We do not sell your information. We share it only:

- **with government agencies,** such as the U.S. Department of Labor, state workforce agencies, USCIS and the U.S. Department of State, as needed to file your applications
- **with service providers** who help us operate, such as hosting, payment processing, e-signature, email and text messaging, only for that purpose
- **with consular and logistics partners** you have engaged us to coordinate, limited to what they need to do that work
- **when required by law,** or to respond to lawful requests, audits or investigations
- **in a business transfer,** such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this Policy

## 4. Worker information

When a grower uploads information about its workers, the grower is responsible for having the right to share it. We use worker information only to provide services to that grower and to meet legal obligations.

## 5. Text messages

If you opt in, we send text messages about invoices that are coming due or past due. Message frequency varies, usually no more than three messages per invoice. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number or text-message opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

## 6. How we protect information

Customer documents are stored in a private, encrypted system. Each customer can see only their own files, and every view and download is logged. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep customer documents for at least 3 years after the end of each certification period, because H-2A rules require records to be available for audits, and longer if needed for an audit, investigation or legal claim. Other information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy.

## 8. Your choices

- **Email:** unsubscribe using the link in any marketing email.
- **Text:** reply STOP.
- **Access and correction:** contact us to review or update your information.
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your information. We will, unless the law requires us to keep it.
- **Cookies:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

## 9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy and will post the new version with a new effective date. For material changes, we will notify account holders by email.
