Most of what H-2A costs is the cost of having a crew: wages, housing, travel and meals. Filing fees are a smaller slice, but they add up. Here is what to budget.

## Government fees

These are the main government fees as of 2025. Fees change, so confirm current amounts with [DOL](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-V/part-655/subpart-B/section-655.163), the [USCIS fee schedule](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055) and the [State Department](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/fees/fees-visa-services.html) before you file.

| Fee | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| DOL certification | $100 plus $10 per worker, capped at $1,000 |
| USCIS petition (Form I-129), named workers, employers with fewer than 25 employees | $545 per petition |
| USCIS petition, named workers, larger employers | $1,090 per petition |
| USCIS asylum program fee | $300 for small employers, $600 for larger ones |
| Customs entry fee (Form I-94) | $24 per worker |
| Visa application fee | $205 per worker |

**Example: a small farm with 6 named workers**

- DOL: $100 + (6 x $10) = $160
- USCIS petition and asylum fee: $545 + $300 = $845
- Entry fees: 6 x $24 = $144
- Visa fees: 6 x $205 = $1,230
- **Total: about $2,379**

Who pays which fee, and when visa and travel costs are reimbursed, follows specific rules. We walk every farm through them before the season.

## The costs that make up most of a season

- **Wages.** You pay the highest of several rates, usually the Adverse Effect Wage Rate (AEWR). See [AEWR explained](/resources/aewr-explained).
- **The three-fourths guarantee.** You must offer at least 75% of the hours in the contract. See [the three-fourths guarantee](/resources/three-fourths-guarantee).
- **Housing.** Free, inspected housing for workers who cannot go home each night. See [housing requirements](/resources/housing-requirements).
- **Travel and meals.** Inbound travel is reimbursed once a worker finishes half the contract, and return travel is paid at the end. See [travel reimbursement](/resources/travel-reimbursement).
- **Meals or a kitchen.** Three meals a day, or free cooking facilities.
- **Workers' compensation** covering the whole contract period.

## Agency fees

Agencies charge for preparing and filing your paperwork, coordinating the consulate and travel, and supporting you through the season.
