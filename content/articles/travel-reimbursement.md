Travel costs are a common source of H-2A back pay, usually because the timing or the amount was off. Here is how it works ([20 CFR 655.122(h)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/chapter-V/part-655/subpart-B/section-655.122)).

## Inbound travel: the 50% point

If you did not pay a worker's travel to your farm in advance, you must reimburse it once the worker completes **50% of the contract period**. That includes:

- **Transportation** from where the worker was recruited, at least the cost of the most economical reasonable fare.
- **Daily subsistence** (meals) for each day of travel.

**Example:** a 20-week contract reaches the 50% point at the end of week 10. Reimbursement is due by then.

Separate federal minimum wage rules can require some travel and visa costs to be repaid in the **first workweek**, if they would otherwise cut a worker's pay below minimum wage. Ask us how this applies to your crew.

## Daily subsistence amounts

As of April 2026, daily subsistence is at least **$16.78 per day**, and up to **$68.00 per day** with receipts ([Cornell Agricultural Workforce Development](https://agworkforce.cals.cornell.edu/2026/05/27/h-2a-may-2026-round-up/)). These amounts change each year, so check the current figures.

## Return travel

When a worker completes the contract, or you let them go without cause, you pay their transportation and daily subsistence home. If they go straight to another H-2A employer, that employer generally takes on the cost instead.

You do not owe return travel to a worker who abandons the job or is fired for cause, as long as you notify DOL and DHS within 2 working days. See [terminations and abandonment](/resources/terminations-abandonment).

## Daily transportation

You must provide **free transportation** between employer-provided housing and the worksite. Vehicles must meet safety standards and be properly insured, and drivers must be licensed. Keep copies of registrations, insurance and driver's licenses.

## Records to keep

- receipts or booking records for each worker's travel
- each worker's 50% date and when reimbursement was paid
- the pay statement showing the reimbursement
- return travel records at the end of the season
