Privacy Policy

Battenkill Ag Services, LLC Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Battenkill Ag Services, LLC (“Battenkill Ag,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and protects information through battenkillag.com and our customer portal (the “Services”).

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • contact details: name, farm name, email, phone, mailing address
  • account information: login credentials
  • business information: farm location, crops, job details, wage and housing information, payroll records
  • documents uploaded to the portal, which may include personal information about workers, such as names, dates of birth, passport details and employment records
  • payment information, processed by our payment processor (we do not store full card or bank account numbers)
  • messages you send us, quote requests and checklist download requests

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and similar usage data, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide H-2A agent services and prepare and submit filings to government agencies
  • operate the portal, including document drop boxes, invoices and payments
  • send service messages, deadline reminders and invoice reminders by email or text
  • respond to quote requests and questions
  • send our newsletter and blog updates, if you’ve downloaded a resource, requested a quote or subscribed (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • maintain security, prevent fraud and improve the Services
  • meet legal, record-keeping and audit obligations

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • with government agencies, such as the U.S. Department of Labor, state workforce agencies, USCIS and the U.S. Department of State, as needed to file your applications
  • with service providers who help us operate, such as hosting, payment processing, e-signature, email and text messaging, and only for that purpose
  • with consular and logistics partners you have engaged us to coordinate, limited to what they need to perform those services
  • when required by law, or to respond to lawful requests, audits or investigations
  • in a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to this Policy

5. Worker information

When a grower uploads information about its workers, the grower is responsible for having the right to share it. We use worker information only to provide services to that grower and to meet legal obligations.

6. Text messages

Mobile numbers and text-message consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

7. Data retention

We keep H-2A records for at least as long as federal record-keeping rules require, generally at least 3 years after the end of the certification period, and longer if needed for an audit, investigation or legal claim. Other information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described here.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

  • Email: unsubscribe using the link in any marketing email.
  • Text: reply STOP.
  • Access and correction: contact us to review or update your information.
  • Deletion: you may ask us to delete your information. We will do so unless we must keep it for legal or record-keeping reasons.
  • Cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy and will post the new version with an updated effective date. For material changes, we will notify account holders by email.
